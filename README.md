# Primitivum

A small browser game: shoot the word PRIMITIVUM to pieces with a bazooka.

**Play:** https://chlupacthebosmer.github.io/primitivum/

Click or tap where you want the rocket to go. A hit blasts a jagged hole out of the letters it reaches, and the
pieces fall and shatter on the floor. A shot that goes cleanly between two letters knocks one of them loose in one piece.
The speaker button (or M) turns sound off; R rebuilds the word.

One self-contained `index.html` with plain canvas code. No build step and no dependencies.
